Environment sheet, original text-to-image request
Create one original landscape 3:2 environment concept sheet for a small 3D browser game's art director. Four illustrated panels in a precisely aligned 2 x 2 grid, full bleed, thin warm ivory gutters, no titles, no letters, no digits or UI anywhere. One consistent built miniature language: a contemporary community music district built of matte fired red clay block, cream limewash, dark indigo ceramic roof, with glossy turquoise glazed entry tiles, oxidized brass railings, and human-scale musicians. Recognizable oversized identity object at every target venue: a bright turquoise circular ceramic plaque with two deep parallel diagonal incisions across its face. Top left: atmospheric orthographic oblique masterplan, a single walkable switchback lane with several individually shaped low cultural buildings, intimate corner studio and much larger sawtooth-roof hall, and tiny foreground figures. Top right: street-level close architectural elevation of the intimate community studio, a wide open indigo door with the large turquoise plaque and warm light, one seated and one standing figure for scale. Bottom left: cutaway interior of that same listening room, visible turquoise door jamb and plaque, one seated cellist on a single step surrounded by seven neighbors in a loose horseshoe, one neighbor uses a wheelchair with an unobstructed route to a sightline. Bottom right: wide immersive view inside a large sawtooth-roof community concert hall, rock trio performing on a wide tiled proscenium, clear drums, guitar, bass and visible performers, dancing audience in the foreground, one open lateral aisle leading to front row, stack of tiered balconies, steady pools of honey light and indigo shadows, turquoise plaque on stage wall. Charm of a well-used place: chipped corners, slip marks, the glaze catches light selectively. Art-directed keyframes, matte gouache over architectural-model lighting, deliberate graphic shapes, sophisticated material palette, strong silhouette and satisfying scale change. Keep generous gutters between all four fully self-contained frames. No overlays, no branding, no lettering, no collage of photography, no logos, no watermark.
